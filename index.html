2Where and how

Popularity estimates use the stores' autocomplete, and Google Play needs search results, and both come through the proxy. Add its URL under Advanced on the Rank check tab. App Store competition data works without it.

Real Apple popularity scores (optional)

If you have Apple's search popularity scores (5 to 100) for this country from Apple Ads or an ASO tool, paste them as keyword, score lines. They replace the estimate for App Store keywords.

  • Popularity (0 to 100) is an estimate from how early the keyword shows up in the store's own search suggestions. It is not search volume, and no free public source gives real monthly volumes. Tiers: 80+ very high, 60+ high, 40+ medium, 20+ low.
  • Difficulty (0 to 100) comes from how big the top 5 apps are (ratings on the App Store, installs on Google Play) and how many of the top 10 titles contain the keyword. Lower is easier.
  • Opportunity = popularity × (100 − difficulty) ÷ 100. Click a column heading to sort; use “track” to add a keyword to the rank checker.
