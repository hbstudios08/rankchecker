Real Apple popularity scores

If you have Apple's search popularity scores (5 to 100) for this country, from Apple Ads or an ASO tool, paste them as keyword, score lines. They replace the estimate for App Store keywords.

  • Popularity (0 to 100) is an estimate based on how early the keyword shows up in the store's own search suggestions. It is not search volume, and no free public source provides real monthly volumes. Tiers: 80+ very high, 60+ high, 40+ medium, 20+ low.
  • Difficulty (0 to 100) reflects how big the top 5 apps are (ratings on the App Store, installs on Google Play) and how many of the top 10 titles contain the keyword. Lower is easier.
  • Opportunity is popularity × (100 − difficulty) ÷ 100. Click a heading to sort, and use + track to add a keyword to the rank checker.
  • Darker cells are better for you: higher ranks, higher popularity, lower difficulty.
